Physics starts in Grade 7

Every Physics 40S student was once in a middle years classroom. This guide walks you through the physics in Manitoba's renewed Grade 7 and 8 science outcomes, with demos, labs and activities that build the foundations senior high physics relies on.

For middle years teachers at any comfort level with physics. About 30 minutes. ★ marks the outcomes that matter most.

1. What changed

3 minutes

The renewed Kindergarten to Grade 10 science curriculum replaces the old cluster units with five strands that run all year. Physics no longer lives in one unit; it shows up across the year as gravity, energy, particles and light.

The renewed documents were posted for voluntary implementation in 2025–26, with a provincial rollout announced for fall 2026. Check your division's timeline.

StrandFor physics, this means
A. Indigenous Knowledge SystemsFirst Nations, Métis and Inuit ways of observing and creating technologies (SCI.7.A.1, SCI.8.A.1).
B. Science IdentityStudents seeing themselves as people who do science.
C. Practical ScienceMeasurement in SI units. Grade 7 measurement includes newtons (N), joules (J) and m/s.
D. Nature of SciencePredictions, fair tests, variables and models: the scientific method.
E. Scientific KnowledgeThe content outcomes, covered in Modules 2 and 3.

Grade 8: OpticsThe electromagnetic spectrum stays, now through solar radiation SCI.8.E.7–E.9. Reflection, refraction, lenses and colour mixing no longer appear.
